Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Task Bloom app and website (the "Service") collect, how we use it and the choices you have. The Service is provided by Acid Soft ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we receive your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your name and profile picture from Google. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your planner content. Tasks, events, routines, checklists and notes you create are stored locally on your device.
  • Location (optional). If you attach a place to a task, the app asks for location permission and uses Google Maps to show and save that place. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Voice input (optional). If you dictate a task, the app uses your device's speech recognition service (for example, Google) to turn speech into text. We do not record or store audio.
  • Usage and device data. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example, screens viewed, app version, device type and approximate region). This helps us fix bugs and improve features.
  • Push notification token. Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a token so we can send notifications.

2. How we use information

  • to provide the app's features, including sign-in, reminders and notifications;
  • to keep your account secure and help you recover access;
  • to understand usage, fix problems and improve Task Bloom;
  • to respond to your messages and requests;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your planner content for advertising.

3. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

We process your data to perform our agreement with you (providing the Service), based on our legitimate interests (keeping the Service secure and improving it), and with your consent where required (for example, location and microphone permissions, which you can withdraw in your device settings at any time).

4. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the Service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Analytics, Cloud Messaging);
  • Google Maps Platform and Google Play services;
  • your device's speech recognition provider, when you use voice input.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is active. Content stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Analytics data is kept according to Firebase retention settings.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app. This removes your account from Firebase Authentication. You can also email us at info@acidsoft.io to request deletion of your account and associated data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Task Bloom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or on this website.
